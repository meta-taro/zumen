  • New year box: odd numbers, and no gaps
  • Allergen flow: never let the path double back
  • Pruning instructions: not which branch, but where to cut it
  • Gill net: the mesh opens because the net is hung short
  • Industrial pattern: cloth cannot be uncut
  • Restorative chart: one number fixes a tooth
  • Every dimension comes from what cattle will not do
  • Three small grids decide the fourth - the cloth is computed
  • Haemocytometer grid: the 0.100mm depth is what turns an area into a volume, so one 1mm square holds exactly 0.1 microlitres
  • Aquarium filter in section: it does not remove the poison, it turns it into a weaker one — and the water must meet the mechanical media before the biological
  • Stitches are specified by number, not by name: 301 rather than "lockstitch" — three digits for the stitch, five for the seam, and the two standards are separate because thread and cloth are decided separately
  • Cloth stretches on the 45 degree bias because nothing stretches: the woven square shears into a rhombus, so at a 120 degree cell the length gains 22.5 percent while the width loses 29.3 percent — it narrows more than it lengthens
