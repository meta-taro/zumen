Retail and logistics

18 drawings in Retail and logistics, each generated from a single YAML file.

  • Assembly line and inspection
  • Stock and reordering
  • A convenience store floor layout: the fixtures, the main aisle, the till and chilled cases, and the path a customer takes from the door
  • Warehouse location addressing
  • Shopping street directory, trade by hatch
  • A food court laid out with 240 seats in the middle and the outlets around the edge, each cuisine distinguished by hatch rather than colour
  • Stadium seating by price band
  • Container stowage: bay, row and tier are the address
  • Camera coverage, out to the distance a face still reads
  • Pallet patterns, and what they do to stability
  • Yardage book: shade the surfaces, write the distances
  • Moving route: the drawing that says whether it fits
  • Truck loading: weight times distance from the axle
  • General arrangement: four drawings, each with its own scale
  • Crane lift plan: the further out, the less it lifts
  • Pallet rack: the truck decides the aisle
  • Baseball infield: every distance starts at the apex of home plate
  • Standard mail is not a size of envelope but the name of four conditions met together — length, width, thickness and weight — and breaking any one of them moves the item to a different price table entirely; the 1cm thickness limit is really the line between what a sorting machine can swallow and what it cannot
