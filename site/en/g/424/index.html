Work and organisations

Qualified invoice (the six required items, with tax rounded once per rate)

A Japanese qualified invoice on one A4 sheet. The tax agency sets no form, only six items, tagged here: issuer name and registration number, date, items with reduced-rate marks, totals per tax rate, tax per rate, and the buyer. A fictional bill shows them. Tax is rounded once per rate per invoice; rounding line by line comes out one yen short. Shop receipts may drop the buyer.

This drawing comes from one YAML file. No shape was moved by hand.
