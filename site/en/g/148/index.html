Egress plan: three lengths answering three questions

What the source says

Three different lengths, three different questions.

Common pathhow far before you can choose between two exits
Dead endhow far into a corridor that goes nowhere
Exit access travelhow far to an exit once you can choose

Sprinklers lengthen all three —— which is why the egress plan and thesprinkler plan get read together（見本 145）.

Occupant load decides how many exits; travel distance decides where.48 people would allow a single exit; the conference room pushes the floorpast 49, so two are required.
