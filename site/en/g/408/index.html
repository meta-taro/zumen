Learning and geometry

Answer sheets for optical mark reading (a fictional form: ID columns, timing marks, good and bad marks)

A fictional machine-read answer sheet. The ID is written in boxes and marked digit by digit, 0 at the top; answers are one oval per question; black timing marks down the edge let the reader count rows, while the ovals are printed in a dropout colour the reader ignores. Beside it: one good mark, six bad ones, common ID mistakes and the pencil rules of two exams.

This drawing comes from one YAML file. No shape was moved by hand.
