What the source says

A deed does not draw the land. It describes a walk.

Start at the point of beginning, take each call in turn - a bearing and adistance - and the last one has to bring you back. The drawing is the checkon the words: if the calls do not close, the description is broken.

A bearing is never over 90 degrees. It is read from north or from south,turned toward east or west, so the quadrant letter carries the rest:N 89°45'00" E is "face north, turn 89 degrees 45 minutes toward east".

This parcel closes to 0.04 ft in 892.90 ft (1 : 20,143) because it wascomputed rather than walked. A field traverse never closes at zero - theratio is how a survey says how well it closed.

1 px = 1 ft. Bearings, distances and monuments are fictional.
