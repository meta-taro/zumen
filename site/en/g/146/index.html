What the source says

One loop of 32. #1 is the upper right third molar, #16 the upper left,#17 the lower left, #32 the lower right —— the numbering walks the arch andcomes back, so a single number fixes a tooth without saying the quadrant.

Surfaces are the other half of the address: M O D (mesial, occlusal,distal) with B/F and L completing the five. "#19 MOD composite" tells anyreader exactly what exists and where.
