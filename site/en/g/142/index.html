The schedule is shaped like the panel.

Odd circuits run down the left, even down the right, exactly as the breakerssit in the enclosure. Phase alternates every row, so a two-pole breakerpicks up A and B and balances itself.

A printed copy lives inside the panel door — NEC 408.4(A) asks for acircuit directory in which every circuit is distinguishable from every other.The VA figures here are connected load; demand factors (NEC Article 220)are applied before sizing the feeder.
