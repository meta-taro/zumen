Instruments and acoustics

Band stage plot and input list: circle numbers on the plot are the console channels

A made-up band's stage plot and input list on one A4 sheet. The 8 by 5 m stage is drawn at 1:60 from the audience side, with stage left and right marked. Every mic and DI is a numbered circle whose number is its console channel, so the 16-row input list and the plot can be checked against each other. Wedges carry their monitor mix numbers, and power drops and stage boxes are marked.

This drawing comes from one YAML file. No shape was moved by hand.
