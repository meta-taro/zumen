Instruments and acoustics

Soprano recorder fingering chart (Baroque and German differ only on F)

Soprano recorder fingerings from low C to high F, each column drawn as one recorder: thumb hole on top, then left hand 1 to 3 and right hand 4 to 7, with 6 and 7 as double holes. Black is closed, white is open, hatched is the half-open thumb. Written in Baroque fingering, with German fingering added beside the two notes where it differs: F and high F. Below, how to tell the two systems apart.
