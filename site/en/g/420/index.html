Manuscript paper (B4, 20 by 20 squares, 8.5 mm squares with 4.5 mm correction strips)

Japanese manuscript paper on B4 landscape to JIS S 5508:2010. 20 by 20 squares read top to bottom, right to left, with 8.5 mm squares and a 4.5 mm correction strip between lines. The standard fixes the sizes and the marking, not how to write. The example shows the usual school rules: title three squares down, name at the bottom, a one-square paragraph indent.

This drawing comes from one YAML file. No shape was moved by hand.
