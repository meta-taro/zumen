Before you install

This is an alpha. It is not code-signed, so your computer will warn you. Please do not rely on it for daily work yet.

Not sure which CPU you have?

  • macOS: Apple menu › About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple silicon; "Processor: Intel…" means Intel.
  • Windows: Settings › System › About. "System type: x64-based" means x64; "ARM-based" means ARM.

Opening it for the first time

  • The run-without-installing builds are the safest bet (zumen-macos-app.tar.gz on a Mac, zumen-windows-*.exe on Windows). They open more reliably than the installers.
  • macOS: if it says the app "is damaged and can’t be opened", right-click zumen in Finder and choose Open.
  • Windows: if it says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info › Run anyway.
