正本に書いてある決まりごと

Spacing is two numbers at once —— the floor area one head is responsiblefor, and how far apart heads may sit. Light hazard allows 225 sq ft and15 ft; this grid uses 12 x 12 ft, so each head carries 144 sq ft.

The wall rule follows from the spacing: no more than half of it, here7 ft 6 in. Widen the grid and the heads move away from the walls as well.

日本語の見本 136（天井伏図）・119（消火器と消火栓）と対。

Figures follow NFPA 13 light hazard, Table 10.2.4.2.1(a) (checked against the 2022 edition; 2025 is the current one). The room and the layout are fictional.
