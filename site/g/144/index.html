車椅子対応の便所（図は器具ではなく、空きでできている）

正本に書いてある決まりごと

The drawing is made of clearances, not of fixtures.

What makes this room work is the empty space: a 60 in turning circle, a60 x 56 in clear floor space at the water closet, and 30 x 48 in at thelavatory. The fixtures are what is left over.

Dimensions follow the 2010 ADA Standards (604 / 609 / 305 / 304).The room and the fixtures are fictional.
