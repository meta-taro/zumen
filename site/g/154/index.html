Fire alarm device plan (fictional)

火災報知の配置（間隔は四角ではなく円）

この図は、下の 1 枚の YAML から描かれています。手で図形を動かしてはいません。

正本に書いてある決まりごと

Spacing is a circle, not a square. The nominal 30 ft is the side of thesquare that fits inside the circle a detector actually covers, so the realreach is 21 ft —— and in a corridor narrow enough to have no corners towaste, heads may sit 41 ft apart. The odd number is the rule working.

日本語の見本 136（天井伏図）と対（同じ天井に、別の決まりで物を置く）。

Devices, spacing and the floor are fictional.

Rules checked against NFPA 72 (2025 ed.), read 2026-10-04 from the copyincorporated by reference: https://archive.org/details/pub.cfr.nfpa.72.202517.7.4.2.3.1 ceilings up to 40 ft: nominal 30 ft, half of it to walls,or every point within 0.7 x 30 ftA.17.7.5.1 not closer than 36 in to a supply diffuser or return air opening(annex: advisory)The 4 in "dead air" corner (17.6.3.1.3.1) is a heat-detector rule; smokedetectors may sit on the ceiling or on a sidewall within 12 in of it(17.7.4.2.1), so it is not listed here.
