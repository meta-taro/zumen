正本に書いてある決まりごと

A fire map is not a picture of the fire.

It is where the day is assigned and where the way out is written. So theline that is not finished is drawn as not finished - solid where it holds,dashed where it does not. A map that shows only completed line tells a crewthe fire is caught.

LCES is not a poster. Lookouts, communications, escape routes and safetyzones are on this sheet, or they do not exist. Divisions are lettered roundthe fire from the anchor point, so a name on the radio is a place on the map.

Every map product carries STANDL-SGD - scale, title, author, north arrow,date, legend, source, grid, datum. IAP maps are drawn at 1:24,000 and carriedin a pocket.

The fire, the divisions and the points are fictional.
